## About

**Devil's Cut** is a small 2D turn-based RPG featuring visual-novel-style story scenes, AP-based combat, supporter choices, item synergies, boss encounters, and multiple progression systems.

This is the very first game I have ever completed.  
It took about three months to bring this project to life.

There were many features and ideas I wanted to include, but handling everything on my own naturally left me with a few regrets. Even so, the journey of building something from scratch and seeing it through to the end was truly rewarding.

As a huge fan of anime and games, I have hidden numerous homages throughout the game.  
I hope they bring a small smile to your face when you discover them.

Although it is far from perfect, I sincerely hope this game brings a little joy to your time.

Thank you so much for playing.

## Features

- 2D turn-based RPG combat

- Visual-novel-style dialogue scenes
- Boss encounters
- Supporter recruit/reject choices
- Item synergies and rarity-based effects
- Player growth and save data
- WebGL, Windows, macOS, and Android builds

## WebGL Save Data Notice

The WebGL version stores save data in your current browser's local site storage.

Your save data may be lost if you delete browser cache/site data, play in incognito or private browsing mode, or use a different browser or device.

Please avoid closing or refreshing the page immediately after saving or updating clear records.

Save data is not shared between different PCs, phones, browsers, or devices.

Important notes:

- Please continue playing in the same browser.
- Incognito/private browsing mode is not recommended.
- Do not delete browser site data if you want to keep your save.
- Avoid closing the tab or refreshing the page immediately after saving.
- If the itch.io project URL or upload path changes, the save location may also change.
- Save data does not automatically transfer to another PC, phone, browser, or device.
